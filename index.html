The idea

Premium patisserie, functional nutrition and circular ingredients in one bite.

Favé was created to bridge the gap between indulgent desserts and better-for-you snacking. It keeps the theatre and richness of a macaron, while adding a stronger nutritional and sustainability story.

Hero product

Hazelnut macaron filled with rich plant-based chocolate mousse.

The first Favé launch combines a chocolate hazelnut pulp macaron shell with a smooth dark chocolate fava bean tofu mousse. A chocolate barrier helps protect the shell from moisture migration, supporting the crisp yet chewy macaron experience.

Circular ingredient model

Partnering with hazelnut milk manufacturers to give pulp a second life.

Favé uses leftover hazelnut pulp from plant-based milk production as a functional macaron shell ingredient. This turns a low-value waste stream into a hero material and makes the sustainability story easy for consumers to understand.

Waste stream utilisationHazelnut pulp is recovered and transformed into the shell.
UK-grown fava beansA local legume base for the chocolate mousse.
Fava circularityFava starch can support PLA packaging, while fibre can be sold as animal feed.
Chilled controlDesigned for 0 to 5°C chilled storage and distribution.
